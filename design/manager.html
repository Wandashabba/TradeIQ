Design decisions

Summary → act → detail

Execution Score is the one number that matters, so it leads with its trend. What needs action today sits directly beneath it; the full KPI ledger follows.

One sheet, not card grids

Content sits on a single rounded sheet — the sign-in screen's structure — with hairline rules for rhythm. Denser, calmer, and cheaper to build than a card per number.

State is never color alone

Severity = dot + word; deltas = sign + figure. Blue stays reserved for brand and interactive elements. Readable in grayscale and for CVD.
